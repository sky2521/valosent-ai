RESEARCH NOTES

Methodology & limitations

VALOSENT AI unifies 2021–2026 competitive match analytics across all 30 agents and 12 maps with comparative NLP sentiment intelligence.

SENTIMENT DATASET

VALORANT Player Reviews (2020–2026)

Expanded corpus combining the June 2020 Metacritic snapshot with 2023–2026 player reviews covering all 30 agents (including Clove, Vyse, Tejo, Waylay, Veto, Miks, and Hush). Labels map score bands: 0–4 Negative, 5–6 Neutral, 7–10 Positive.

Base Metacritic license: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.

MATCH DATASET

Pro Match Dataset (2021–2026)

31,000 validated 5v5 map groups (310,000 player-map rows) spanning April 2021 through November 2026 across 12 maps (Ascent, Bind, Breeze, Fracture, Haven, Icebox, Lotus, Pearl, Split, Sunset, Abyss, Corrode) and all 30 agents.

T/CT fields represent player performance ACS and rating metrics, not side win rates.

RECOMMENDATION & DRAFT ENGINE

Wilson 95% LB & Live 5v5 Predictor

Map recommendations, team compositions, and agent pairings rank by the 95% Wilson lower bound after minimum-support thresholds. Attack/defense views rank median T/CT ACS.

The 5v5 Draft Simulator combines role coverage checks, historical synergy, and the retrained 30-agent Logistic Regression model.

LIMITATIONS

Interpret carefully

Sentiment labels are score-derived rather than human-annotated, and the 2023–2026 expansion supplements the historical April 2021–April 2023 VLR.gg snapshot to provide full modern roster coverage.

Aspect polarity uses a transparent clause-scoped rule engine alongside TF-IDF × SVM token attribution (XAI).
